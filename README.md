 Kaadu 🌿 — Biodiversity Explorer PWA
A window into the natural world.
Kaadu (കാട്, Malayalam for "forest") is an installable Progressive Web App for exploring forests
and ecosystems around the world and the species that have actually been recorded in each one — real
photographs, real conservation status, real sourced facts. Nothing is invented: where a fact isn't
available from a real source, the app says so.
World → country → forest/ecosystem → species → real photo + honest facts.
Features
🗺️ Interactive, hand-built world map — SVG + d3-geo, fully offline, no map-tile service.
🌍 105 catalogued forests and ecosystems across 59 countries, organised by continent.
🐾 Live species lists for each forest, fetched from real naturalist observations — never a
fixed or capped list.
🔍 Filter by 9 groups (mammals, birds, reptiles, amphibians, fish, insects, plants, fungi,
other), each with a live count, plus a "threatened only" toggle.
🔎 Unified search — places instantly, species live, from one box.
📸 Species pages combining four public data sources: real photographs and sound recordings
(iNaturalist), descriptions (Wikipedia), measurements (Wikidata), and distribution by country
(GBIF) — each fact labelled with its source, or shown as "Not available" rather than guessed.
🏷️ IUCN conservation status, with a visual category ladder.
📱 Installable as an Android app: standalone landscape window, its own icon, a custom opening
screen, and offline access to pages you've already visited.
↩️ Smart back-navigation — returning from a species restores your filter, pagination, and
scroll position.
